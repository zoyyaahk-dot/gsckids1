WHO WE ARE

A Place Where Children Thrive

Global Kids is an educational initiative of Shaheen Group of Institutions dedicated to providing children with a strong foundation for their academic and personal growth.

We believe that education is not limited to textbooks and examinations. Children learn best when they are given the opportunity to ask questions, discover new ideas, communicate freely and develop their unique talents.

Our approach combines quality education, modern learning practices, discipline, creativity and values to create a positive environment for every student.
